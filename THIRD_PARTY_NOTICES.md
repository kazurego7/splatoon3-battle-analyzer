## Optional weapon icon input

`app/scripts/sync-weapon-catalog.mjs` and `app/scripts/sync-rule-icons.mjs` download weapon and rule icons from the user-selected source, currently the [Splatoon3 攻略＆検証 Wiki icon page](https://wikiwiki.jp/splatoon3mix/icon).
